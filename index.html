Download map tiles by county. They stay on your device — no random failures, no re-downloading every time you go out.

Tap the map, add details and photos, save. Works with zero signal. Your data survives app close, phone reboot, or dead battery.

Draw polygons to mark sectors — to search, active, or searched. See at a glance where your team has been and where they haven't.

Record where your team actually went. Start a track, move through the field, stop when done. Distance, duration, and route — all saved offline.

Why BlocNav Exists

Slow networks are worse than no network.

Most apps treat connectivity as binary — online or offline. In the field, you're usually somewhere in between: your phone shows bars, but nothing loads. BlocNav was designed for this "deceptive connectivity" — local data is always available first, and the system never blocks your maps waiting on a network that isn't really there.
